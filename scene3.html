On the road

Trip length changes what efficiency looks like.

As you scroll from your car's preferred trip length, you can compare it to other trip lengths to see how petrol, diesel, and hybrid shift under each driving context.

How to read the movement

Each dot is one trip. Dots farther right had better observed mileage. Each row is one fuel type. Dot color shows the efficiency band, and the chart updates as the road moves from your preferred drive to various other trips.

Long trips in focus

Each row is a fuel type. Each dot is one long trip.

Read left to right for lower to higher mileage.
